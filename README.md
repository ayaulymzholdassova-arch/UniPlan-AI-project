# UniPlan AI – An AI-Powered Study Planner for University Students

**1. Problem and Target Users**

University students often struggle to manage multiple assignments, exams, deadlines, and personal schedules. They may forget important deadlines or have difficulties planning their study time. Our target users are university students, especially students who want to organize their academic activities more effectively.

**2. AI Feature and Evaluation**

The main AI feature is an intelligent academic assistant. Students can upload course syllabi, assignment descriptions, and study materials. AI will extract important deadlines, identify tasks, and generate a personalized weekly study plan.

We will measure performance using:
- Deadline extraction accuracy (target: 90%).
- Task classification accuracy (target: 85%).
- User satisfaction (target: 80%).

**3. Agentic Tools**

- **Google Gemini API:** AI-powered text analysis and study planning (free tier, subject to usage limits).
- **Google Calendar API:** Automatically creates calendar events after user confirmation (free).
- **Firebase:** User authentication and database (free tier).

**4. MVP and Stretch Goals**

MVP:
- Student registration and login.
- Uploading syllabi and assignment files.
- AI deadline extraction.
- Personalized weekly study plans.
- Google Calendar integration.

Stretch Goals:
- AI chatbot for academic questions.
- Progress tracking and smart study recommendations.

**5. Data and APIs**

We will use university syllabi, assignment descriptions, and user-provided academic information. The project will use Google Gemini API, Google Calendar API, and Firebase. Personal student information will be protected.
